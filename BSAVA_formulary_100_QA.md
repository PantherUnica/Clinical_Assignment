# BSAVA Small Animal Formulary, 6th edition: 100 questions and answers

Each answer is taken from the book's own text for the topic named in brackets. Doses are as printed in the book; check current product data before clinical use.

**1. What is the dose of acepromazine (ACP) for sedation in dogs and cats?**  
_[Acepromazine]_  
0.01-0.02 mg/kg slowly i.v.; 0.01-0.05 mg/kg i.m. or s.c.; or 1-3 mg/kg p.o. Sedation is unreliable when ACP is used alone, and combining it with an opioid improves sedation (neuroleptanalgesia).

**2. When should acepromazine be avoided?**  
_[Acepromazine]_  
In hypotension due to shock, trauma or cardiovascular disease, in animals under 3 months old and in animals with liver disease. Large breeds are very sensitive when dosed on a mg/kg basis, so use the low end of the range. In Boxers, use low doses (0.01 mg/kg i.m.) or avoid it, because fainting and syncope from sinoatrial block can occur.

**3. What is the dose of amlodipine for systemic hypertension in cats?**  
_[Amlodipine]_  
0.625-1.25 mg per cat p.o. q24h. The dose may be increased slowly or the frequency raised to q12h if needed, and blood pressure monitoring is essential. It appears safe even with concurrent renal failure.

**4. What is the oral dose of amoxicillin for dogs and cats?**  
_[Amoxicillin]_  
10 mg/kg p.o. q8-12h. Parenterally it is 7 mg/kg i.m. q24h, or 15 mg/kg i.m. q48h for depot preparations.

**5. What is the oral dose of amoxicillin/clavulanate in dogs and cats?**  
_[Amoxicillin/Clavulanate]_  
12.5-25 mg/kg (combined) p.o. q8-12h. The parenteral dose is 8.75 mg/kg (combined) i.v. q8h or i.m./s.c. q24h.

**6. What is the dose of apomorphine to induce vomiting in a dog, and which route works best?**  
_[Apomorphine]_  
40-100 µg/kg s.c. or i.m.; the s.c. route is more effective. Emesis generally occurs rapidly, within a maximum of 10 minutes.

**7. Is apomorphine recommended to induce emesis in cats?**  
_[Apomorphine]_  
No. It is not recommended in cats; the formulary notes that xylazine is a potent emetic in cats and at least as safe.

**8. When is induction of emesis contraindicated?**  
_[Apomorphine]_  
When a strong acid or alkali has been ingested, because of the risk of further damage to the oesophagus.

**9. How is atipamezole dosed to reverse medetomidine in a dog?**  
_[Atipamezole]_  
Five times the previous medetomidine dose (in mg/kg) i.m., which is an equal volume to the medetomidine given. If medetomidine was given at least an hour earlier, the dose can be halved and repeated if recovery is slow.

**10. What is the i.v. dose of atropine for bradyarrhythmias in dogs and cats?**  
_[Atropine]_  
0.02-0.04 mg/kg i.v. Low doses may exacerbate bradycardia, but repeating the dose usually increases heart rate.

**11. Why should atropine be avoided in glaucoma?**  
_[Atropine]_  
Because atropine increases intraocular pressure, so it is contraindicated in glaucoma and lens luxation. It also reduces tear production, so it should be avoided in keratoconjunctivitis sicca.

**12. What is the dose of azathioprine in dogs with immune-mediated disease?**  
_[Azathioprine]_  
2 mg/kg p.o. q24h until remission, then 0.5-2 mg/kg p.o. q48h, often with corticosteroids. It is not recommended in cats.

**13. What is the dose of benazepril for heart failure in dogs?**  
_[Benazepril]_  
0.25-0.5 mg/kg p.o. q24h.

**14. What is the dose of buprenorphine in dogs and cats?**  
_[Buprenorphine]_  
20 µg/kg i.v., i.m. or s.c. q6h for both species. In cats it is also well tolerated and effective given sublingually.

**15. Why is buprenorphine not recommended for premedication when potent opioids will be needed during surgery?**  
_[Buprenorphine]_  
Because it is a partial agonist that antagonizes the effects of full opioid agonists such as morphine and fentanyl.

**16. What is the antitussive dose of butorphanol in dogs?**  
_[Butorphanol]_  
0.05-0.1 mg/kg i.m. or s.c., or 0.5-1 mg/kg p.o. q6-12h.

**17. What is the dose of cabergoline for false pregnancy in a bitch?**  
_[Cabergoline]_  
5 µg/kg p.o. q24h for 4-6 days. Controlling aggression-related signs may need dosing for 2 weeks.

**18. Why must cabergoline not be given to pregnant bitches?**  
_[Cabergoline]_  
Because it may cause abortion. It should also not be combined with hypotensive drugs or used in hypotensive animals.

**19. What is the starting dose of carbimazole (Vidalta) for a hyperthyroid cat?**  
_[Carbimazole]_  
15 mg per animal p.o. q24h, unless total thyroxine is below 100 nmol/l, in which case start at 10 mg q24h. Adjust in 5 mg increments and do not break the tablets.

**20. What is the perioperative dose of carprofen in dogs?**  
_[Carprofen]_  
4 mg/kg i.v. or s.c. before surgery or at induction, which should give analgesia for up to 24 hours. It can be continued orally at 4 mg/kg/day, and after 7 days may be reduced to 2 mg/kg/day depending on response.

**21. How is carprofen licensed for use in cats?**  
_[Carprofen]_  
Only as a single perioperative dose of 4 mg/kg i.v. or s.c., given before surgery or at induction, for postoperative pain.

**22. What is the dose of cefovecin (Convenia) and how often can it be repeated?**  
_[Cefovecin]_  
8 mg/kg s.c., equivalent to 1 ml per 10 kg of reconstituted drug. It may be repeated after 14 days, up to three times.

**23. In which animals should cefovecin not be used?**  
_[Cefovecin]_  
Small herbivores such as rabbits and guinea pigs, and dogs and cats under 8 weeks old. Its use should also be avoided in pregnant and lactating animals.

**24. What is the dose of chlorambucil for immune-mediated disease in dogs and cats?**  
_[Chlorambucil]_  
1-2 mg/m2 p.o. q24h. It should only be considered where more established therapies such as prednisolone and azathioprine have failed.

**25. What is the oral dose of ciclosporin for atopic dermatitis in dogs?**  
_[Ciclosporin]_  
5 mg/kg p.o. q24h until signs are controlled, then q48h as a maintenance dose.

**26. How is ciclosporin ointment used for ocular disease in dogs, and how quickly does it work?**  
_[Ciclosporin]_  
Apply about 0.5 cm of ointment to the affected eye q12h. Improvement may take 2-4 weeks, occasionally up to 12 weeks.

**27. What is the dose of clindamycin for toxoplasmosis?**  
_[Clindamycin]_  
25 mg/kg p.o. daily in divided doses, in both dogs and cats.

**28. Why must clindamycin be avoided in rabbits and guinea pigs?**  
_[Clindamycin]_  
Lincosamides cause a potentially fatal clostridial enterotoxaemia in rabbits, guinea pigs, chinchillas and hamsters.

**29. What is the dose of clomipramine in dogs and cats?**  
_[Clomipramine]_  
Dogs: 1-2 mg/kg p.o. q12h. Cats: 0.25-1 mg/kg p.o. q24h. It is licensed for separation-related disorders in dogs alongside a behaviour modification plan.

**30. Which drug class must not be given with clomipramine?**  
_[Clomipramine]_  
Monoamine oxidase inhibitors, such as selegiline.

**31. What is the dose of clopidogrel for thromboprophylaxis in cats?**  
_[Clopidogrel]_  
18.75 mg per cat p.o. q24h. It is used to prevent thrombosis in cats with significant heart disease.

**32. What is the oral dose of cyclophosphamide for lymphoid neoplasia in dogs?**  
_[Cyclophosphamide]_  
Generally 50 mg/m2 p.o. q48h or on 4 consecutive days a week, or 200-250 mg/m2 i.v. every 3 weeks.

**33. What are the doses of dexmedetomidine for premedication and deep sedation?**  
_[Dexmedetomidine]_  
Premedication: 5 µg/kg i.v., i.m. or s.c. with an opioid. Deep sedation: 10-15 µg/kg i.m. with an opioid. These apply to dogs and cats.

**34. What are the doses of medetomidine for premedication and deep sedation?**  
_[Medetomidine]_  
Premedication: 10 µg/kg i.v., i.m. or s.c. with an opioid. Deep sedation: 20-30 µg/kg i.m. with an opioid.

**35. In which patients should medetomidine not be used?**  
_[Medetomidine]_  
Animals with cardiovascular or other systemic disease. Use in geriatric patients is not advisable, and it should not be used in pregnancy.

**36. What is the dose of diazepam for status epilepticus in dogs?**  
_[Diazepam]_  
A bolus of 0.5-1 mg/kg i.v., or intrarectally if there is no venous access. Onset is 2-3 minutes i.v., so it can be repeated every 10 minutes up to three times if there is no effect.

**37. What is the dose of midazolam for emergency seizure control?**  
_[Midazolam]_  
A bolus of 0.2-0.3 mg/kg i.v., or intrarectally if there is no venous access, repeated every 10 minutes up to 3 times if there is no effect.

**38. What is the oral tablet dose of digoxin in dogs?**  
_[Digoxin]_  
5.5-11 µg/kg p.o. q12h for tablets (5-8 µg/kg q12h for the elixir). For dogs over 22 kg, 0.22 mg/m2 is a more accurate regimen.

**39. What is the oral dose of diltiazem in cats?**  
_[Diltiazem]_  
0.5-2.5 mg/kg p.o. q8h, or one 10 mg tablet q8h for cats of 3-6.25 kg.

**40. When is diltiazem contraindicated?**  
_[Diltiazem]_  
In second or third degree heart block, marked hypotension, sick sinus syndrome, or severe left ventricular dysfunction.

**41. What is the dose of doxycycline in dogs and cats, and how should it be given?**  
_[Doxycycline]_  
10 mg/kg p.o. q24h with food. It should not be given if there is evidence of oesophagitis or dysphagia.

**42. What is the dose of enalapril for cardiac disease in dogs?**  
_[Enalapril]_  
0.25-1 mg/kg p.o. q12-24h. Doses up to 3 mg/kg are recommended for hypertension.

**43. What is the dose of enrofloxacin in dogs and cats?**  
_[Enrofloxacin]_  
5 mg/kg s.c. or i.v. q24h, or 2.5 mg/kg p.o. q12h, or 5 mg/kg p.o. q24h.

**44. Why are fluoroquinolones relatively contraindicated in growing dogs?**  
_[Enrofloxacin]_  
Because cartilage abnormalities have been reported in young dogs and rabbits (but not cats).

**45. What is the fenbendazole dose for Angiostrongylus vasorum in dogs?**  
_[Fenbendazole]_  
50 mg/kg p.o. q24h for 7 days (also used for Oslerus osleri).

**46. How is fenbendazole given to pregnant bitches?**  
_[Fenbendazole]_  
25 mg/kg p.o. q24h from day 40 of pregnancy until 2 days after whelping, roughly 25 days.

**47. What is the dose of firocoxib in dogs, and can it be used in cats?**  
_[Firocoxib]_  
5 mg/kg p.o. q24h, with or without food, for osteoarthritis. It must not be used in cats.

**48. What is the dose of fluoxetine in dogs and cats?**  
_[Fluoxetine]_  
Dogs: 1-2 mg/kg p.o. q24h. Cats: 0.5-1.0 mg/kg p.o. q24h. A history of seizures is a contraindication.

**49. What is the fomepizole protocol for ethylene glycol toxicity in dogs?**  
_[Fomepizole]_  
20 mg/kg i.v. over 30 minutes, then 15 mg/kg i.v. slowly at 12 and 24 hours, then 5 mg/kg i.v. q12h until ethylene glycol is negligible or the dog has recovered. It is not effective in cats.

**50. What is the dose of furosemide for cardiogenic or pulmonary oedema in dogs?**  
_[Furosemide]_  
1-2 mg/kg i.v. or i.m. q2-6h (up to 4 mg/kg in acute short-term situations), then 2-4 mg/kg p.o. q8-12h once stable. Maintenance is often 1-4 mg/kg p.o. q12h, using the lowest dose that controls signs.

**51. What is the starting dose of lente insulin for diabetes mellitus in dogs?**  
_[Insulin]_  
0.25-0.5 IU/kg s.c. q12-24h for dogs over 25 kg, or 0.5-1 IU/kg for dogs under 25 kg, adjusted by monitoring clinical signs, urine, blood glucose and fructosamine.

**52. How is soluble insulin given i.m. for diabetic ketoacidosis in dogs?**  
_[Insulin]_  
0.2 IU/kg soluble insulin i.m. initially, then 0.1 IU/kg i.m. q1h. An i.v. infusion of 0.025-0.06 IU/kg/h is an alternative.

**53. What is the MAC of isoflurane in dogs?**  
_[Isoflurane]_  
1.2% expired concentration maintains surgical anaesthesia in 50% of dogs. Unpremedicated patients need 3-5% for induction, and other anaesthetic agents and opioids reduce the requirement.

**54. What is the dose of itraconazole and how long is treatment?**  
_[Itraconazole]_  
5 mg/kg p.o. q24h in dogs and cats. Treatment may need 4-20 weeks depending on culture results.

**55. Why is ivermectin not recommended in collies?**  
_[Ivermectin]_  
Collies and related breeds are sensitive, so MDR-1 gene testing should be considered before use in these breeds.

**56. What is the ivermectin dose for sarcoptic mange in dogs?**  
_[Ivermectin]_  
200-400 µg/kg p.o. or s.c. every 2 weeks for 4-6 weeks.

**57. What is the ketamine dose for perioperative analgesia by infusion in dogs?**  
_[Ketamine]_  
A 0.25 mg/kg loading dose, then 10 µg/kg/min intraoperatively and 2-5 µg/kg/min postoperatively.

**58. What is the dose of lactulose for constipation in dogs and cats?**  
_[Lactulose]_  
Dogs: 5-25 ml p.o. q8h, adjusted to give two or three soft stools a day. Cats: 0.5-5 ml p.o. q8-12h.

**59. How is lactulose used for acute hepatic encephalopathy in dogs?**  
_[Lactulose]_  
As a retention enema of 18-20 ml/kg of a solution of 3 parts lactulose to 7 parts water, held for 4-8 hours.

**60. What is the dose of levothyroxine for hypothyroidism, and how is it monitored?**  
_[Levothyroxine]_  
0.02-0.04 mg/kg/day (or 0.5 mg/m2 daily), given once or divided twice daily. Serum T4 is checked before dosing and 4-8 hours after dosing.

**61. What is the maximum total dose of lidocaine for local anaesthesia in dogs?**  
_[Lidocaine]_  
4 mg/kg. Lidocaine solutions containing adrenaline must not be given i.v.

**62. Can lidocaine be given to cats as a perioperative constant rate infusion?**  
_[Lidocaine]_  
No. The formulary says not to give lidocaine to cats by continuous rate infusion during the perioperative period.

**63. What is the mannitol dose for acute glaucoma or cerebral oedema?**  
_[Mannitol]_  
1-2 g/kg i.v. infusion over 30 minutes, withholding water for the first few hours. It may be repeated 2-4 times over the next 48 hours while monitoring for dehydration.

**64. What is the antiemetic dose of maropitant in dogs?**  
_[Maropitant]_  
1 mg/kg s.c. q24h or 2 mg/kg p.o. q24h. For motion sickness it is 8 mg/kg p.o. q24h for a maximum of 2 days.

**65. Is maropitant authorized for cats, and what dose is suggested?**  
_[Maropitant]_  
It is not authorized in cats, but 0.5-1 mg/kg p.o. or s.c. q24h is likely to reduce emesis from 2 to 24 hours after dosing.

**66. What is the dose of meloxicam in dogs?**  
_[Meloxicam]_  
An initial 0.2 mg/kg s.c. or p.o., followed by a maintenance dose of 0.1 mg/kg p.o. q24h.

**67. What is the injectable dose of meloxicam in cats?**  
_[Meloxicam]_  
0.3 mg/kg s.c. as a single preoperative injection lasting 24 hours. Continuing with the oral suspension after the injection was not recommended at the time of writing.

**68. How long a wash-out period is advised before switching from one NSAID to another?**  
_[Meloxicam]_  
1-2 weeks. NSAIDs should also not be given within 24 hours of other NSAIDs or glucocorticoids.

**69. What is the dose of methimazole (Felimazole) in cats?**  
_[Methimazole]_  
2.5 mg per cat p.o. q12h. It must not be used in pregnant or lactating queens.

**70. What is the dose of metoclopramide in dogs and cats?**  
_[Metoclopramide]_  
0.2-0.5 mg/kg i.m., s.c. or p.o. q6-8h, or 1-2 mg/kg i.v. over 24 hours as a slow constant rate infusion.

**71. When should metoclopramide not be used?**  
_[Metoclopramide]_  
When GI obstruction or perforation is present, or for more than 72 hours without a definitive diagnosis.

**72. What is the metronidazole dose for protozoal infections in dogs?**  
_[Metronidazole]_  
The higher dose of 25 mg/kg p.o. q12h. The general dose is 15-25 mg/kg p.o. q12h.

**73. What is misoprostol used for, and what is the dog dose?**  
_[Misoprostol]_  
Protection against NSAID-induced gastric ulceration. Dogs: 2-7.5 µg/kg p.o. q8-12h. It must not be used in pregnant animals as it can cause abortion.

**74. What is the induction dose of mitotane for pituitary-dependent hyperadrenocorticism in dogs?**  
_[Mitotane]_  
30-50 mg/kg p.o. q24h to effect (generally 7-10 days), then weekly to fortnightly as required.

**75. What is the constant rate infusion dose of morphine in dogs?**  
_[Morphine]_  
0.15-0.2 mg/kg/h i.v.

**76. What is the dose of naloxone and how long does it last?**  
_[Naloxone]_  
0.015-0.04 mg/kg i.v., i.m., s.c. or intratracheally. Onset i.v. is very rapid but the duration is short (30-40 minutes), so repeat doses or an infusion may be needed.

**77. What is the dose of omeprazole in dogs and the maximum treatment duration?**  
_[Omeprazole]_  
0.5-1.5 mg/kg i.v. or p.o. q24h for a maximum of 8 weeks.

**78. Can paracetamol be used in cats?**  
_[Paracetamol]_  
No. Cats lack the glucuronyl transferase enzymes needed to metabolize it. The dog dose is 10 mg/kg p.o. q12h.

**79. What is the starting dose of phenobarbital in dogs with epilepsy?**  
_[Phenobarbital]_  
2-3 mg/kg p.o. q12h.

**80. How is phenobarbital loaded i.v. for status epilepticus in a dog not already on it?**  
_[Phenobarbital]_  
12 mg/kg i.v., then if needed two further doses of 3 mg/kg i.v. at 20-minute intervals, to a maximum total of 18 mg/kg. Wait 20 minutes between doses because CNS levels take that long to respond.

**81. What is the dose of phenylpropanolamine for urinary sphincter incompetence?**  
_[Phenylpropanolamine]_  
1 mg/kg p.o. q8h or 1.5 mg/kg p.o. q12h. Incontinence may recur if doses are delayed or missed.

**82. What is the dose of pimobendan and how should it be given relative to food?**  
_[Pimobendan]_  
0.1-0.3 mg/kg p.o. q12h, given one hour before food.

**83. In which cardiac conditions is pimobendan contraindicated?**  
_[Pimobendan]_  
Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, and conditions where cardiac output cannot be increased by stronger contraction, such as aortic stenosis.

**84. What is the maintenance dose of potassium bromide and the target serum level?**  
_[Potassium bromide]_  
20-40 mg/kg p.o. q24h. The therapeutic serum concentration is 1.0-2.0 mg/ml, and steady state may take 3-4 months because the half-life is over 20 days.

**85. Why is potassium bromide not used in cats?**  
_[Potassium bromide]_  
It can cause severe coughing due to eosinophilic bronchitis, which may be fatal.

**86. What is the oral dose of praziquantel in dogs and cats?**  
_[Praziquantel]_  
5 mg/kg p.o. in both species. The spot-on dose is 8 mg/kg, and the injectable dose is 3.5-7.5 mg/kg i.m. or s.c.

**87. What is the immunosuppressive dose of prednisolone in dogs?**  
_[Prednisolone]_  
1.1-3.3 mg/kg p.o. q12h, tapering to 0.5-2.2 mg/kg q48h.

**88. What is the induction dose of propofol in premedicated versus unpremedicated dogs?**  
_[Propofol]_  
Unpremedicated: 6-7 mg/kg i.v. Premedicated: 1-4 mg/kg i.v.

**89. How is selamectin used to treat sarcoptic mange in dogs?**  
_[Selamectin]_  
Minimum 6 mg/kg applied on three occasions at 2-week intervals. It is not for use in animals under 6 weeks old.

**90. What is the dose of selegiline in dogs and for how long?**  
_[Selegiline]_  
0.5-1 mg/kg p.o. q24h for a minimum of 2 months.

**91. What is sildenafil used for in dogs and at what dose?**  
_[Sildenafil]_  
Pulmonary arterial hypertension, at 0.3-3 mg/kg p.o. q6-8h.

**92. What is the dose of spironolactone, and when is it contraindicated?**  
_[Spironolactone]_  
2-4 mg/kg p.o. q24h in dogs and cats. It is contraindicated in conditions with hyperkalaemia, such as Addison's disease, anuria and acute renal failure.

**93. What is the dose of sucralfate in dogs and cats?**  
_[Sucralfate]_  
Dogs up to 20 kg: 500 mg p.o. q6-8h; over 20 kg: 1-2 g p.o. q6-8h. Cats: 250 mg p.o. q8-12h. A perforated ulcer is a contraindication.

**94. What is the oral dose of tramadol in dogs and cats?**  
_[Tramadol]_  
Dogs: 2-5 mg/kg p.o. q12h. Cats: 2-4 mg/kg p.o. q12h. Owners should be told there may be a slightly increased risk of seizures.

**95. What is the dose of trilostane for hyperadrenocorticism in dogs?**  
_[Trilostane]_  
2-12 mg/kg p.o. once daily, starting at the low end of the range and increasing gradually. Renal and hepatic insufficiency should be excluded first.

**96. What is the vincristine protocol for transmissible venereal tumour?**  
_[Vincristine]_  
0.025 mg/kg i.v. every 7 days for 4 weeks.

**97. What is the vitamin K1 protocol for second-generation anticoagulant (brodifacoum) toxicity in dogs?**  
_[Vitamin K1]_  
Initially 5 mg/kg s.c. in several sites, then 2.5 mg/kg p.o. q12h for 3 weeks, then re-evaluate coagulation status. Restrict activity for 1 week after treatment.

**98. What is the oral acetylcysteine loading dose for paracetamol poisoning?**  
_[Acetylcysteine]_  
140 mg/kg p.o. as a loading dose (followed by 70 mg/kg doses). Alternatively it is given by i.v. infusion starting with 150 mg/kg in 200 ml 5% glucose over 15 minutes. It should be used immediately if ingestion occurred within 24 hours.

**99. What is the dose of aglepristone for pregnancy termination in the bitch, and up to when can it be used?**  
_[Aglepristone]_  
10 mg/kg s.c. q24h for two doses, up to 45 days after mating. Partial abortion can occur in up to 5% of cases, and a check 10 days after treatment is recommended.

**100. What is the dose of desmopressin for coagulopathies such as von Willebrand's disease in dogs?**  
_[Desmopressin]_  
1-4 µg/kg i.v. once, diluted in 20 ml saline and given over 10 minutes.
