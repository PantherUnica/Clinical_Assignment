**2. When should acepromazine be avoided?**
_[Acepromazine]_

**Book answer:** In hypotension due to shock, trauma or cardiovascular disease, in animals under 3 months old and in animals with liver disease. Large breeds are very sensitive when dosed on a mg/kg basis, so use the low end of the range. In Boxers, use low doses (0.01 mg/kg i.m.) or avoid it, because fainting and syncope from sinoatrial block can occur.

**AI answer:** Acepromazine is a phenothiazine tranquilizer. [1] Based on the provided evidence, acepromazine/phenothiazine derivatives should be avoided within 1 month of worming with an organophosphate anthelmintic. [2] Acepromazine has historically been avoided in epileptic animals because it was previously cautioned to lower the seizure threshold and cause seizures. [2] However, the provided evidence states that there is no clinical evidence to support this claim, and some studies have shown acepromazine has some anticonvulsant activity. [2] The provided evidence does not cover any other situations in which acepromazine should be avoided.
